DAY 4

Free Time & Weekends

日期：待补充 · 主题：兴趣与周末
Free timeWeekends
In my free time, I like reading novels and watching movies.
空闲时，我喜欢读小说和看电影。
They help me relax after a busy day.
它们帮助我在忙碌一天后放松。
It depends. Sometimes I read novels, and sometimes I just relax.
看情况。有时我读小说，有时就休息。
自然表达：It depends. 后面应说明“取决于什么”或直接补充不同情况；单独说 On the weekend. 不是完整说明。

DAY 8

Plans, Changes & Possibilities

日期：2026-07-27 · 主题：计划、变化与可能
Changed plansFuture plans

句型卡片

I was going to interview someone, but he didn't have time.
我本来打算采访一个人，但他没有时间。
I was going to meet a friend for coffee, but something came up.
我本来打算和朋友喝咖啡，但临时有事。
I was going to rest at home, but my parents called me to help them out.
我本来打算在家休息，但父母叫我去帮忙。
I'm planning to go to a concert next weekend.
我计划下周末去听一场演唱会。
I might go swimming.
我可能会去游泳。（还不确定）

重点词汇与句型

表达意思使用提示
was going to本来打算过去的计划后来改变了
something came up临时有事自然说明计划为何改变
help someone out帮某人一把help my parents out
be planning to计划做某事较明确的未来计划
might可能表达尚未确定的安排
work on着手做、忙于I'm working on my article.

我的完整表达

I'm planning to go to a concert next weekend, and I might go swimming.
表达对比：I was going to… 说过去未按原计划发生的事；I'm planning to… 说较明确的计划；I might… 说还不确定的可能。
DAY 9

Talking About a Busy Day

日期：2026-07-28 · 主题：忙碌的一天与当下感受
Daily lifePast tense

句型卡片

Today, I was really busy.
今天我真的很忙。
I had a lot of work.
我有很多工作要做。
I also went swimming.
我还去游泳了。
So now I feel a bit tired.
所以我现在感觉有点累。

重点词汇与表达

表达意思使用提示
really busy非常忙really 加强语气
a lot of work很多工作work 在这里是不可数名词
went swimming去游泳了go swimming 的过去式
a bit tired有点累a bit + 形容词
so now所以现在自然连接过去经历和当前感受

我的完整表达

Today, I was really busy. I had a lot of work. I also went swimming. So now I feel a bit tired.
时态提醒：描述今天已经完成的事情时，用过去式 was / had / went；描述此刻的感受时，用现在时 I feel…。
DAY 10

Talking About KTV & Pop Music

日期：2026-07-29 · 主题：KTV、流行歌与喜欢音乐的原因
KTVPop musicPast tense

句型卡片

Today, I was really excited because I went to KTV and sang lots of pop songs with my friends.
今天我很兴奋，因为我和朋友一起去 KTV 唱了很多流行歌。
But I still have some work to finish, so I need to work hard tomorrow.
但我还有一些工作要完成，所以明天仍然要努力工作。
I usually sing pop songs because these singers inspired me when I was younger.
我通常唱流行歌，因为这些歌手在我年轻时给过我启发。
And I still enjoy their music now.
现在我仍然喜欢他们的音乐。

重点词汇与表达

表达意思使用提示
went to KTV去了 KTVgo 的过去式是 went
sang pop songs唱了流行歌sing 的过去式是 sang
have some work to finish有一些工作要完成work 是不可数名词
inspire激励、启发inspired me when I was younger
still enjoy仍然喜欢still 放在实义动词 enjoy 前

我的完整表达

Today, I was really excited because I went to KTV and sang lots of pop songs with my friends. But I still have some work to finish, so I need to work hard tomorrow.

I usually sing pop songs because these singers inspired me when I was younger, and I still enjoy their music now.
时态提醒：描述今天已经发生的事情，用过去式 went / sang；描述现在仍存在的工作和喜好，用现在时 have / need / enjoy。
